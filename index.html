i.  skin ; body ; ocean

stop. do not turn from the flame in the mountain the palms who ripped the peaks

in two purple petals on bare skin shoes so worn the heels crack leaving a path of prints so aching so wide unfillable even when tried until exhaustion settles into marrow the dog who loves is also one who bites the tired mouth still seeks out something warm as blood gun powder is a familiar taste & red-hot steel yet the mind stubborn clings to sunshine clear skies a wavering mirage of picket fences & a belly full only fools dare to dream only fools see an ocean in a body and see foreign only fools call a step between worlds split open different dangerous ending so learn to swallow the earth whole remember the shoes remember the hands remember
